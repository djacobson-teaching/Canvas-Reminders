Canvas Course Readiness Scale

How we'll rate what we see

A 0 to 4 scale for how clearly and consistently Canvas works as an intentional extension of your classroom. Level 3 is the expected target. Tap a level to explore it.

Published content only

We see what students see. Unpublished modules, pages, and assignments aren't part of the review.

Highest level, consistently

We start with your homepage and Modules, then sample Assignments, Pages, and Quizzes. The rating is the highest level the evidence supports across that sample.

One piece of the picture

This is one Canvas scale. Your broader instructional practice stays part of the Instructional Framework observation.

Walkthrough look-fors

What observers are looking for

Grouped by the five YUHSD Instructional Framework commitments. Each area lists what we check, plus a quick example of what it could look like.

Canvas walkthroughs are snapshots of how you design learning environments. Instructional observations capture how that design comes to life, and how students experience it.

Quick self-check

Where does your course land?

Thirteen yes-or-no questions built from the readiness scale. Answer honestly and you'll get a level estimate plus your next move.
